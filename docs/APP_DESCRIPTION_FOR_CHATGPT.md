# TALMA Practice Pal - Application Description

## What It Is

**TALMA Practice Pal** is an English language learning platform designed for middle school students (grades 7-8) to practice speaking, vocabulary, and sentence construction through interactive games and activities.

## Who Uses It

**Students**: Practice English through games, complete lessons, record themselves speaking, and track their progress.

**Teachers/Admins**: Create and manage lessons, add vocabulary, configure games, and monitor student activity.

## What It Does

The app helps students learn English by providing:

1. **Vocabulary Practice**: Students see vocabulary words with images, hear pronunciations, and can view translations in Hebrew or Arabic.

2. **Sentence Completion**: Students fill in blanks in sentences (e.g., "My favorite color is ___") by selecting from multiple choice options. They hear the complete sentence pronounced correctly and can record themselves repeating it.

3. **Matching Games**: Memory-style games where students match vocabulary pairs (images, words, or audio).

4. **Flashcard Games**: Students see an image or hear audio, then select the matching word from options.

5. **Spelling Games**: Students practice spelling vocabulary words with audio support.

6. **Sentence Builder**: Students arrange words in the correct order to form sentences.

7. **True/False Questions**: Students answer true/false questions about vocabulary or concepts.

8. **Grammar Exercises**: Focused practice on sentence clauses and grammar concepts.

## How It Works

### For Students

1. Students browse courses (organized by grade level) and select a lesson.
2. Each lesson starts with a vocabulary presentation showing the words they'll practice.
3. Students then work through various activities (games, prompts, exercises) in sequence.
4. Activities provide immediate feedback (correct/incorrect) and track scores.
5. Students can hear pronunciations, record themselves, and see their progress.

### For Teachers/Admins

1. Create courses and lessons with titles, descriptions, and cover images.
2. Add vocabulary words (individually or via CSV import).
3. The system automatically:
   - Translates words to Hebrew and Arabic
   - Finds or generates images for vocabulary
   - Creates audio pronunciations
4. Create activities:
   - Sentence completion prompts with multiple choice options
   - Configure games (matching, flashcards, spelling, etc.)
   - Set up grammar exercises
5. Organize activities within lessons and publish for students.

## Key Features

- **Multilingual Support**: Vocabulary automatically translated to Hebrew and Arabic
- **Audio Everywhere**: All vocabulary and sentences have audio pronunciations
- **Visual Learning**: Images accompany vocabulary words
- **Speaking Practice**: Students can record themselves (privacy-controlled)
- **Progress Tracking**: Scores and completion tracked for all activities
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Works on phones, tablets, and computers
- **Self-Paced**: Students work through lessons at their own speed

## Content Organization

- **Courses**: Top-level containers (e.g., "Grade 7", "Grade 8")
- **Lessons**: Individual practice sessions within courses
- **Activities**: Games and exercises within lessons (vocabulary, prompts, matching, flashcards, etc.)
- **Vocabulary**: Words that appear across activities in a lesson

## What Makes It Unique

- **Pre-generated Audio**: All audio is created ahead of time, so students get instant playback without waiting
- **Automatic Content Creation**: Teachers can import vocabulary via CSV, and the system automatically adds translations, images, and audio
- **Privacy-First Recording**: Student recordings can be kept entirely local (in the browser) without uploading to servers
- **Multiple Game Types**: Seven different activity types keep practice varied and engaging
- **Bilingual Support**: Hebrew and Arabic translations help students understand vocabulary in their native language

## Typical Use Case

A teacher creates a lesson about "Colors" for Grade 7:
1. Adds vocabulary: red, blue, green, yellow (via CSV or manually)
2. System automatically adds Hebrew/Arabic translations, finds images, generates audio
3. Creates sentence prompts: "My favorite color is ___" with color options
4. Adds a matching game using the color vocabulary
5. Publishes the lesson
6. Students access the lesson, see vocabulary, complete sentence prompts, play matching games, and record themselves saying sentences

## Current Capabilities

✅ Vocabulary recognition and practice  
✅ Sentence construction practice  
✅ Speaking practice (recording)  
✅ Listening practice (audio playback)  
✅ Multiple game types for varied practice  
✅ Progress tracking  
✅ Mobile support  

## Current Limitations

❌ No pronunciation evaluation (students can record but don't get feedback on accuracy)  
❌ No adaptive difficulty (all students see the same content)  
❌ No writing practice (except spelling)  
❌ No reading comprehension passages  
❌ No multiplayer or competitive features
